Our Story

Akrogiali Restaurant has been part of Kokkari, Samos since 1984, inspired by the sea, the island's traditions and the warmth of Greek hospitality.

Today, the next generation continues this journey, combining the authentic character of our family restaurant with a fresh and contemporary approach to Mediterranean cuisine.

We work with local ingredients and traditional recipes, while adding our own creative touch. From handmade Samian dolmadakia and seafood cooked with local flavours to our homemade breads, sauces and desserts, every dish is prepared with care.

Our philosophy is simple: good food, genuine hospitality and the Aegean Sea at your table.
